# GreenGrid_CHE110_Project
B2B Carbon Footprint Optimization &amp; Audit File
GreenGrid
B2B Carbon Footprint Optimization & Audit
Tool
Course: Environmental Studies (CHE110)
Objective
GreenGrid gives small and medium-sized enterprises
(SMEs) a lightweight, accessible tool to
measure, report, and reduce their day-to-day
operational carbon emissions — no consultant,
no expensive software, no specialized training
required.
Problem
Large corporations have sustainability teams and
enterprise carbon-accounting software. SMEs —
the businesses that make up the vast majority of a
local economy — usually have neither the
budget nor the staff for that, so their emissions go
unmeasured and unmanaged by default.
